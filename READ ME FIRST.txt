BELLECORD

This is a streamlined version of my app, BelleCord: Fluxer and Discord in one window.
If you find any bugs, tell me (screenshots help a lot).

HOW TO START IT
1. Unzip this folder somewhere it won't get moved.
2. Install Node.js (the LTS version) from nodejs.org.
3. Start it:
   Windows: double-click "START (Windows).bat".
   Mac: right-click "START (Mac).command", choose Open, then Open again (only the first time).
   Linux: open a terminal in this folder and run  ./"START (Linux).sh"
   The first time takes a few minutes.
4. Sign in to Fluxer and Discord. (Only use one of them? The first time it opens, pick "Only Discord" or
   "Only Fluxer" and the other one is hidden. You can change that in the dashboard's App section.)
When there's a new version, an Update button shows up at the top of the app. Click it and it restarts updated.

WHAT'S IN IT
- New to it? Press the 🎮 at the top for a quick tutorial. It walks you through everything, and finishing it
  gets you the BelleCord Pilot badge (turn it off in Game badges if you don't want it).
- What's new: the top of the dashboard shows what came in every update.
- Make the dashboard yours: press Organize at the top right of it to move sections around, hide the ones
  you don't use, and add them back later.
- Fluxer servers on the left, Discord servers on the right.
- Top left: the dashboard (right-click to change its picture). Top right: your DMs.
- Ctrl+1 Fluxer, Ctrl+2 Discord, Ctrl+3 both, Ctrl+4 dashboard (Cmd on Mac).
- Back and forward like a browser: the arrows at the top, your mouse's side buttons, or Alt+Left / Alt+Right
  (Cmd+[ / Cmd+] on Mac).
- T, P and X in the title bar: TikTok, Pinterest and X.
- Closing the window keeps it running in the tray (you can turn that off on the dashboard).
- Animations: Off, Smooth or Ultra, in the App section of the dashboard.
- Computer running slow? The dashboard's Performance section shows what BelleCord is using and has presets
  (Balanced, Light, Battery saver) to make it lighter.
- Watching a stream and switching sides? It keeps playing in a small window in the corner. Drag it,
  resize it from its top-left corner, or double-click it to go back.
- Want to share it? "Send BelleCord to a friend" in the App section gives you a link and a message to send.

THE DASHBOARD
Inbox, voice channels, who's online, today's stats, countdowns, skins, game badges and themes.
Game badges fill in from your real profile for Overwatch, Minecraft, Roblox, Dota 2, the chess sites and more.

GOOD TO KNOW
- Pinterest: Google sign-in doesn't work in the app. Use the QR code or your email and password.
- The app changes how Discord looks, which Discord's rules technically don't allow (same as BetterDiscord or
  Vencord themes). It only changes looks.
